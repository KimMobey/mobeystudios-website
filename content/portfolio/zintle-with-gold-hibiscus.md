{{< poem title="The Oldest Daughter is Zintle" >}}
Who builds a house of colour
Who paints the doors of freedom?
When I wash the flowers and faces from my brush,
And stretch these muscles filled with clay
Can I be just a sister or a daughter?
Or do you only see my dreams
And paint me with them?
